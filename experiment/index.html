Category Learning Experiment

Welcome! In this experiment, you will learn to categorize simple shapes into two categories: Category A and Category B.

How it works:

  • You'll see shapes that vary in shape (circle or triangle), color (red or blue), and size (small or large)
  • Your job is to learn which category each shape belongs to
  • After each guess, you'll receive immediate feedback
  • There is a pattern to learn, but it may take some time to figure out

Practice Round:

We'll start with 8 practice trials to get familiar with the task.
